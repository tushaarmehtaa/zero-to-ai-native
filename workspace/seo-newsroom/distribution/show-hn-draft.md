## Founder's comment (post within 5 minutes of submitting)

```
Hey HN, I'm Tushar. I built Zero to AI-Native because the best explanations
of AI are written by the people building it, and they're scattered across
lab blogs, system cards, arXiv, docs, and lectures. I wanted a maintained
path through the ones actually worth reading.

It's a curated catalog of 124 primary-source reads from 32 sources
(labs, researchers, institutions, no rehosts or secondhand explainers),
sequenced into an 8-module curriculum from foundations through prompting,
retrieval, agents, evals, production, and safety.

The curation rule is simple: every source has to pass a 5-question gate
(transfer, evidence, originality, shelf life, placement) before it's
added, and the bias is to keep the catalog smaller, not bigger. The full
gate is in CURATION.md if you want to see the actual bar.

It's open source. If something's missing or wrong, I'd genuinely like to
know: https://github.com/tushaarmehtaa/zero-to-ai-native/issues
```
