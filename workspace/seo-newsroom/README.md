# Zero to AI Native SEO newsroom

## Constitution

Zero to AI Native is the maintained, primary-source path from understanding AI to building production AI systems. Search work should expose and maintain that learning structure. It must not turn the project into an AI-news blog or a thin link directory.

### Evidence rules

1. Prefer official documentation, original authors, papers, system cards, courses, repositories, and firsthand AI & Weekends material.
2. Discovery surfaces identify leads; they are not sufficient evidence.
3. Separate live observation, repository intent, platform data, and inference.
4. Never invent demand, rankings, traffic, indexation, workshop experience, or outcomes.
5. A source must pass transfer, evidence, originality, shelf-life, and placement checks.

### Search surface

Priority order: curriculum modules, substantive topic hubs, genuinely reordered audience paths, firsthand workshop guides, then periodic update notes. Never create one page per catalog link.

### Candidate states

`discovered -> researching -> needs_owner -> ready -> writing -> qa -> deployed -> verified -> measuring -> improve|hold|consolidate|kill`

Additional exits: `rejected`, `watchlist`, `social_only`, `update_existing`.

### Scoring

- learner relevance: 25
- identifiable search/discovery job: 15
- original contribution: 20
- primary or firsthand evidence: 20
- curriculum/catalog fit: 10
- distribution potential: 5
- durability: 5

Scores of 0–39 are rejected, 40–59 watchlisted, 60–74 routed to catalog/social/existing-page maintenance, 75–84 require a research brief, and 85–100 are merely eligible for reviewed production.

### Publishing gate

The hard ceiling is one new indexable URL per Asia/Kolkata calendar day; expected cadence is at most one per week initially. This is a ceiling, never a quota. Every URL needs distinct learner intent, original judgment, adequate evidence, internal links, metadata, technical verification, and human review.

Scheduled work is report-only. It cannot publish, deploy, commit, request indexing, post, contact anyone, or mutate external systems.

### Explicit rejections

Reject daily AI news, release rewrites, listicles, thin source/company pages, generic explainers, secondhand paper summaries, generated tutorials, keyword variants, and pages claiming to teach what they only link to.

### Checkpoints and kill criteria

- Day 7: verify production availability, rendering, discovery links, sitemap, and canonical behavior.
- Day 30: inspect Search Console indexation, relevant impressions, and queries.
- Day 60: look for relevant discovery, GitHub, curriculum, workshop, or distribution signal.
- Day 90: improve, consolidate, hold, or kill each experiment.

Reduce new-page investment if ten qualified pages have negligible relevant impressions after 90 days, queries are irrelevant, no meaningful project outcome emerges, pages cannibalize each other, firsthand evidence cannot be sustained, or maintenance harms curriculum clarity.
