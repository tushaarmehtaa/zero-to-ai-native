- [Automation's Early Footprint](https://cohere.com/blog/automations-early-footprint), Cohere Labs, page-stated September 3, 2026, by Zanele Munyikwa, Campbell Lund, Marzieh Fadaee, Aidan Peppin and Thomas Euyang. Placement: Foundations or Safety & Governance, understand level, builder and decision-maker audience — to be settled in a production session.

Admission gate on Orchard. Transfer: train and evaluate inside the real harness, not a stand-in, and expect the gap between the two to matter. Evidence: named authors, three benchmark families with numbers, a resolving public repository with trainer and eval code, published trajectory datasets. Originality: open recipes at this layer are rare. Shelf life: tied to a framework, which is the main risk. Placement: Agents or Evals, build level.
